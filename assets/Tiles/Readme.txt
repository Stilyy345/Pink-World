Zro Dfects – Realistic Animated Platformer Water Tileset (Starter Pack)

A huge thank you for downloading my pixel tile asset pack — I really appreciate your interest and support, it really means a lot and highly appreciated.

🎨 What’s Included:
- There are different animated water style tiles to choose from to suit your taste and style which includes:
  All tiles are 16x16, with 22 colors to choose from plus an additional 1-bit tile set.
  24 Animated tiles which includes bubbles (semi see through water) (per each color)
  24 Animated tiles which includes bubbles (solid water) (per each color)
  12 Animated tiles which are without bubbles (semi see through water) (per each color)
  12 Animated tiles which are without bubbles (solid water) (per each color)
  24 Animated tiles which includes bubbles (semi see through water) cartoon style, no wave lines (per each color)
  24 Animated tiles which includes bubbles (solid water) cartoon style, no wave lines (per each color)
  12 Animated tiles which are without bubbles (semi see through water) cartoon style, no wave lines (per each color)
  12 Animated tiles which are without bubbles (solid water) cartoon style, no wave lines (per each color)
  cartoon style may work well if used in HD style games 
  24 animated tiles in 1-bit which includes bubbles
  12 animated tiles in 1-bit are without bubbles
- There are 4 separate individual animated tile sets to suit 1 tile, 2 tiles, 3 tiles and 4 tiles wide, and are all created and animated differently to suit each width, they are designed to double up if you wish to extend, for example, using the 4 tiles wide option, can become 8 tiles wide or even 12 tiles by repeating the 4 tiles next to the first 4 tiles without any editing.
- One tile sets have 4,329 total tiles across all colors
- Two tile sets have 8,613 total tiles across all colors
- Three tile sets have 12,897 total tiles across all colors
- Four tile sets have 17,181 total tiles across all colors
- The overall total tiles comes to 43,020 tiles, (this includes 2,000+ unique tiles)
- Each tile sets are surrounded by a red grid for easy visuals and accessibility, though some tiles are repeated to keep things neat and organized, but duplicate tiles are not counted in the above amounts
- Clean PNG files with transparent backgrounds
